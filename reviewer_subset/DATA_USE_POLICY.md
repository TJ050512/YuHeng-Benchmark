# Data use policy

This reviewer artifact contains sensitive social content and is provided only for defensive safety research, artifact inspection, and reproducibility review.

Users must not:

- attempt to identify or contact original platform users;
- use the data to harass, profile, or target individuals or groups;
- use it to generate harmful content or evade safety systems;
- treat benchmark labels as legal judgments or deployable moderation policy;
- redistribute source-platform personal data discovered during review.

Automated privacy filtering is not a guarantee of complete anonymization. Stop distribution and report the affected `global_id` if a residual identifier is discovered.

No broad license to underlying source-platform media is implied by this research release.
