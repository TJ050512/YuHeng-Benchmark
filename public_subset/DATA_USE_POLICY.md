# Data use policy

This public subset contains sensitive social content and is provided for defensive safety research, artifact inspection, and reproducibility checks.

Users must not:

- attempt to identify or contact original platform users;
- use the data to harass, profile, or target individuals or groups;
- use it to generate harmful content or evade safety systems;
- use it for training or tuning systems evaluated on YuHeng;
- treat benchmark labels as legal judgments or deployable moderation policy;
- redistribute source-platform personal data found in the release.

Automated privacy filtering reduces the risk of disclosing identifiers. If you find a residual identifier, stop distributing the affected record and email its `global_id` to [the corresponding author](mailto:qfang@bupt.edu.cn). Keep the identified material out of public reports.

No broad license to underlying source-platform media is implied by this research release.
